Privacy notice

Site notice updated October 11, 2026.

Your choices

Optional analytics are off until you choose to allow them. Use the Privacy choices button on any page to accept, reject, or change your selection. Rejecting analytics does not prevent you from reading the site or using its contact links. This site saves your selection in this browser’s local storage. Clearing site data removes that choice. If storage is unavailable, your choice applies only to the current page.

Turning off an active optional service reloads the page so its scripts stop running. The site also attempts to remove accessible Google Analytics cookies. This does not delete information already sent to a provider or remove cookies on other domains.

Analytics

If you allow analytics, this site loads Google Analytics and Metricool to understand visits and site interactions. Those providers can receive information about the page, browser, device and visit, and may use cookies or similar technology. Site events include contact and outbound-link interactions, form success and playback of self-hosted video. These analytics scripts do not load before your choice.

Contacting Anthony

The contact form sends the information you enter to the configured Google Apps Script endpoint to handle your message. Choosing to send a message is separate from allowing analytics. Email links open your email application. Do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request.

The site requests fonts from Google Fonts. Its images and videos are served as site assets. Links to Facebook, Strava and other websites take you to services with their own privacy practices.

Questions

For questions about information you submit, contact anthonybrown7557@gmail.com.

Hosting and service providers

Serving a page requires network requests. Hosting and delivery providers can process connection information such as IP addresses and requested URLs. These requests are separate from the optional analytics controlled here.

Provider settings and server-side storage are not controlled by this browser panel. For questions about access, retention or deletion, use the contact route above. See Google’s privacy policy for Google services.
