The site answers on two hostnames — www.dapenguincycling.com 301s to the apex — and every page is also reachable at its `/name.html` and (on GitHub Pages) extensionless `/name` form. Without this tag a search engine has to guess which spelling is the real one and splits the page's signals across them. `absolute_url` builds it from `url` in _config.yml, which is the apex, so the tag, the sitemap and the redirect all agree. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} Social card. Every page shared the one brand image, so a link to the Rides page previewed identically to a link to Contact. A page can override with `social_image` and `social_image_alt` in its front matter. Two constraints on any replacement. It must be JPG or PNG — Facebook, LinkedIn and X do not reliably read AVIF or WebP, which rules out most of images/ — and it must be exactly 1200x630, because the width and height below are declared to the scrapers and a mismatch makes them re-crop or skip the image. Generate one rather than pointing at a content photo: ffmpeg -i source.jpg -vf "scale=1200:-1,crop=1200:630:0:(ih-630)*0.35" -q:v 3 images/social-.jpg The 0.35 anchors the crop above centre, which keeps heads and finish arches in frame where a straight centre crop cuts them. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign social_image = page.social_image | default: "/images/dapenguin-social.jpg" -%} {%- assign social_alt = page.social_image_alt | default: "DaPenguin — a penguin in a Jamaica cycling kit riding a road bike" -%} {% include structured-data.html %} {% include analytics.html %} {%- comment -%} Fonts as a plain stylesheet instead of Webflow's WebFont.load, which pulled a render-blocking script from ajax.googleapis.com and then asked for Open Sans three times over and Bebas Neue at four weights it does not have.
